All controls & flight rules +

Solo: Space to jump / deploy; ← and → to steer. Player 1: W to jump / deploy; A and D to steer. Player 2: ↑ to jump / deploy; ← and → to steer.

Pause: P or Escape. Restart: R. Sound: M. Two-player races are first to two wins. Matching outcomes in the same simulation step are a draw; replay that round.

Solo uses a fixed course for each difficulty. Records are kept on this device and separated by keyboard, touch, and camera steering. Camera controls are optional: hand tracking runs locally, and Space or the on-screen button still controls your jump and chute.
